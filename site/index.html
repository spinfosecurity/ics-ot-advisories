How to use this

For plant engineers, OT defenders, and integrators who need a fast answer to “is this my gear?”

  1. Search your vendor or product family.
  2. Start with Critical and High.
  3. Open the CISA page for affected versions and vendor fixes.
  4. Subscribe to RSS if you want updates in a reader or ticket queue.

This page gathers public CISA CSAF documents. It does not replace vendor bulletins or your change-control process. Always verify products and versions on the official advisory before patching or isolating systems.

Where the data comes from

Plain sourcing so you know what you’re looking at.

  • Official CISA CSAF OT feed (TLP:WHITE).
  • Updated weekly on a gentle schedule—we avoid hammering blocked cisa.gov RSS endpoints.
  • Every row links to the canonical CISA web advisory.
  • Open source: github.com/spinfosecurity/ics-ot-advisories.
